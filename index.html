§02 / RESEARCH

What I work on

My dissertation develops observability-informed adaptive Gaussian-mixture filtering for cislunar relative navigation using only line-of-sight measurements. The problem is hard for three reasons: range is instantaneously unobservable from bearings, the CR3BP dynamics deform Gaussian uncertainties, and observability is both geometry- and time-varying. I work on Lie-derivative and empirical-Gramian observability analyses, square-root unscented filters, and adaptive mixture management.
